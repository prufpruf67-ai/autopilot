Autopilot

A private publishing tool for two independent YouTube channels.

Autopilot is a small command-line tool that runs only on its owner's computer. It helps the owner publish original animated YouTube Shorts to the two channels they own:

  • Wibolo: wordless cartoons for kids aged 4–7.
  • You Are Here: animated "what would you do?" scenarios.

What it does

  1. Checks every finished video: resolution, loudness, black frames, and a required licence list for every asset.
  2. Waits for the owner's manual approval. Nothing is uploaded without it.
  3. Uploads the approved video to the owner's own channel through the YouTube API Services and schedules its publication.

Autopilot is not a public service: it has no other users, does not access anyone else's channel and does not collect any data from viewers.
