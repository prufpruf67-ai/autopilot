Privacy Policy

Autopilot · effective 25 October 2026

Autopilot is a private tool operated by the owner of the Wibolo and You Are Here YouTube channels ("we") to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channels. This policy explains what information Autopilot uses and how.

Use of YouTube API Services

Autopilot uses the YouTube API Services. By using Autopilot you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

Who uses Autopilot

Autopilot is used only by its owner, for the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts and does not access other people's channels.

What information we access

  • Google sign-in tokens for our own channels, created when we authorise Autopilot with Google OAuth. They allow uploads (scope youtube.upload) and reading our own channel's ID and name (scope youtube.readonly).
  • Our own channel's ID and name, used only to make sure each video is uploaded to the correct channel.
  • The status of videos we uploaded (processed, scheduled, rejected), read back after upload.

Autopilot does not collect, request or process any information about viewers, subscribers or commenters, including children. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising identifiers.

How information is stored and shared

Tokens, channel IDs and the IDs of our uploaded videos are stored only in local files on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any server other than Google's, and they are not sold, shared with or disclosed to any third party.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while Autopilot is in use and are deleted from the computer when it is no longer needed. Upload records are kept for at most 12 months. You can ask us to delete any stored data through the contact link below. We will do so within 7 days.

Revoking access

Access granted to Autopilot can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
